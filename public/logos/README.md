# Landing page company marks

The book uses black SVG symbols on transparent backgrounds. The `*-color.svg`
variants are used only in the “Add your accounts” illustration on its dark panel.
They retain the brand colors, including Tiger's yellow disc and the white neutral
bars from Longbridge's dark-background treatment. Wordmarks and app-icon tiles
are omitted.

- `ibkr.svg`: symbol geometry from [IBKR's logo](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/images/common/logos/ibkr/ibkr.svg).
- `moomoo.svg`: orange symbol paths from [moomoo's logo](https://cdn.futustatic.com/moomoo_common/dist/img/logo-3bd67..svg), recolored black.
- `tiger.svg`: tiger symbol from [Tiger Brokers' logo](https://c1.itigergrowtha.com/portal5/static/media/tigerbrokers.en.color.f885f7f5.svg), without its enclosing circle.
- `longbridge.svg`: bar geometry recreated from [Longbridge's header logo](https://assets.wbrks.com/assets/logo/light/sg.png).
- `endowus.svg`: ribbon geometry recreated from [Endowus' favicon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61263092cbd1b46804b2dcb7/6877582eb37a547b79393ed0_favicon%20256x256.png).
- `coinhako.svg`: central symbol path from the [Coinhako SVG asset](https://2fasvg.github.io/assets/img/logo/coinhako.com/coinhako.com.svg).
